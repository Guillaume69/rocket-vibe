# Provider marks

The server rail shows a provider's mark on the tile of an account whose server
has no icon of its own (`node scripts/generate-brand-tiles.mjs` renders them into
`apps/desktop/crates/rv-core/assets/brands/` and `apps/mobile/assets/brands/`).
They identify the service an account belongs to (nominative use); they are the
trademarks of their owners, and RocketVibe is not affiliated with any of them.
